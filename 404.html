Heritage worn every day. A studio in Guwahati weaving the golden thread of Assam into clothes you’ll want to live in.

Every Heeya piece starts on a handloom in Assam. We work with artisans to reinterpret the state’s three great silks, keeping the craft and making room for what’s new.

i.

Muga মুগা

The golden silk, found only in Assam and GI-tagged. Its natural sheen needs no dye and grows richer with age.

ii.

Eri এৰী

Called the ahimsa silk, because it is spun only after the moth has left its cocoon. Warm and matte, soft as wool.

iii.

Mulberry পাট

Assam’s Pat silk: lustrous, smooth and fine. It is the base for most of our woven-motif mekhela sadors.

Mother & daughter

Two generations, one loom.

Reinterpreting Assam’s Muga, Eri and Mulberry silks, and keeping its artisans weaving.

Founder Mitali Baruah Bora began her career in journalism with The Indian Express and All India Radio, then turned to her first love: textiles. Today her daughter Mallika Bora brings a fresh, innovative eye to the design of every mekhela sador.

Together they blend traditional craftsmanship with a contemporary sense of style, and they work to empower the artisans and women behind each piece.
